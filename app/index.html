Wordsy for Android

A free daily word game by Life Guru. Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries. A new word every day, the same for everyone.

Get it on Google Play

Free · Android · No sign-up needed. Prefer your browser? Play on the website.

How it works

  1. Type any 5-letter word and tap Enter.
  2. After each guess the squares show how close you are.
  3. Solve it in 6 tries, then share your result with friends.

Good to know

One daily puzzle, new at midnight (India time), plus Unlimited practice. Hints are available inside the game. See the full rules, the FAQ and our privacy policy for the app.
