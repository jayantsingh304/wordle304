How to play Wordsy

Guess the hidden 5-letter word in six tries. After every guess, the colours tell you how close you are. Here is everything you need to know.

1. Start the game

Open WhatsApp and send hi to +91 99015 45349, or tap Play on WhatsApp. Then type any 5-letter word and send it. That is your first guess.

2. Read the colours

After each guess, every letter gets a colour:

  • 🟩 Green: the letter is in the word and in the right spot.
  • 🟨 Yellow: the letter is in the word, but in a different spot.
  • ⬜ Grey: the letter is not in the word (or you already found all its copies).

Example: the secret word is TABLE. Guess CRANE and you get:

CRANE
TABLE

The first row says: C and R are not in TABLE, A is in the word but in the wrong spot (TABLE has its A in second place), N is not in the word, and E is in the right place. The second row is the winning guess.

3. Use all six guesses

You have six tries. Every guess must be a 5-letter word. If you send the wrong number of letters, Wordsy tells you and the guess is not used up. Win in six or fewer and your game is a win; run out of guesses and the game shows you the word.

4. Repeated letters

If a word has two of the same letter, they are scored carefully: a letter can be matched only as many times as it appears in the answer. Our guide to repeated letters has worked examples.

5. Two ways to play

  • Daily word: one puzzle a day, the same for everyone, new at midnight India time. It counts towards your streak.
  • Unlimited: random words, as many as you like, once you have finished the daily word.

Your first game is a friendly, easy word so that you can win it. Read how Wordsy picks the daily word for more.

6. Hints

Stuck? After your first guess, tap 💡 Hint or type hint. Wordsy reveals one letter in its correct spot. Each word has a small number of hints, and your first word has unlimited hints.

7. Commands

TypeWhat it does
hintReveal one letter of the word
statsShow your games, win %, streak and guess distribution
shareGet a spoiler-free result card to send to friends
giveupEnd the game and show the word
menuShow the main menu
rules or ?Show the instructions again

You can type commands with or without a slash, for example /hint. If you are mid-game, any 5-letter word is treated as a guess.

8. Streaks and sharing

Win the daily word on consecutive days to build a streak. After a game, send share to get a result card with coloured squares and no letters. See streaks, stats and sharing.
