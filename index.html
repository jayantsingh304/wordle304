Wordsy, right inside WhatsApp

A daily word game by Life Guru. Guess the hidden 5-letter word in 6 tries. A new word every day, the same for everyone. No app to install.

Play on WhatsApp

or send hi to +91 99015 45349

How to play

  1. Send hi to start, then type any 5-letter word.
  2. After each guess the squares show how close you are.
  3. Solve it in 6 tries, then forward your result to friends.
🟩 Right letter, right spot
🟨 In the word, wrong spot
⬜ Not in the word

Two ways to play

Daily word: one puzzle a day, the same for everyone, new at midnight (India time).
Unlimited: practise as much as you like.
