About Wordsy

Wordsy is a free daily 5-letter word game that you play inside WhatsApp. It is made by one person, in Bengaluru, India.

What Wordsy is

You get six guesses to find a hidden five-letter word. After each guess, coloured squares show which letters are in the right place, which are in the word but misplaced, and which are not in the word at all. There is a new word every day, the same for everyone, and an Unlimited mode for when you want more. See how to play for the full rules.

Why it lives in WhatsApp

Most of us already open WhatsApp every day. Putting a word game there means there is nothing to download, no account to create and no new app to remember. You message the game, it replies, and your progress is saved against your number. The game uses the official WhatsApp Business Platform.

Who makes it

Wordsy is built and run by Jayant Singh, trading as Life Guru, an individual based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. It is a small independent project, not a large company. The game, this website and every guide on it are written and maintained by the same person.

How we write our guides

The guides section is original writing about 5-letter word puzzles. We do not copy other sites. Where a guide gives numbers, such as which letters are most common, we counted them ourselves from the word lists the game uses, and the examples of scored guesses come from the game's own scoring code, so they match what you see in play.

What we believe

  • The game should be free. There is no paywall, no sign-up and nothing locked behind a payment.
  • Short and friendly. A daily puzzle should take minutes, not hours.
  • Respect your data. We keep only what the game needs. You can erase it any time by sending delete my data. See the privacy policy.
  • Be honest about money. Wordsy is supported by voluntary donations and by ads on this website. We say so openly on the FAQ and in the disclaimer.

Not connected to other games
