Privacy Policy: Wordsy

← Back to Wordsy

This policy covers the Wordsy word game operated by Jayant Singh (Life Guru), played by sending messages to our WhatsApp number. It applies only to the game.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the profile name WhatsApp shares with us.
  • The messages you send to the game (your guesses and commands).
  • Your game history and statistics: words guessed, wins, streaks, dates played.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos or any other WhatsApp data.

How we use it

Only to run the game: to reply to you, keep your progress and show your stats. We count how many people play and how often a sponsor message is shown or clicked, as totals only. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising profiles, or share it with sponsors.

Who processes it

Messages are delivered through the WhatsApp Business Platform by Meta Platforms, under WhatsApp's business terms. Game data is stored on our own server.

Advertising and cookies

This website shows ads from Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies let it and its partners show you ads based on your visits here and elsewhere. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Payments

If you donate, the payment is processed by Razorpay under Razorpay's privacy policy. We receive only the amount, the currency, the payment status and Razorpay's payment ID; we never see or store card, UPI or bank details.

How long we keep it

Game data is kept while you play. You can delete it at any time (see below).

Deleting your data

Send delete my data to the game's WhatsApp number. Your games and statistics are erased immediately and you'll get a confirmation.

Contact

Questions? Message us on the game's WhatsApp number or write to jayant.singh@lifeguru24.in. More on the contact page.
