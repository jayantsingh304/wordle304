Cookie policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how cookies and similar technologies are used on lifeguru24.in (the Wordsy website). The Wordsy game itself runs inside WhatsApp and does not use cookies. For how we handle your game data, see the privacy policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They are used to remember settings, measure how a site is used and show advertising.

Cookies we set

This website does not set cookies of its own, and we do not run our own analytics or tracking scripts.

Cookies from advertising

We show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies, for example to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure how ads perform and, unless you opt out, show ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. This is the standard way advertising works on the web, and it is controlled by those providers, not by us.

  • Google's advertising cookies let Google and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites.
  • You can read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

How to control cookies

  • Personalised ads: turn them off in Google's Ads Settings.
  • Other ad networks: opt out of many third-party advertisers at aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Your browser: every major browser lets you block or delete cookies in its settings. If you block all cookies, this website will still work; you may just see less relevant ads.

Where the law requires it, you may be shown a choice about cookies and personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time.

Changes

We may update this policy if our use of cookies or advertising changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
