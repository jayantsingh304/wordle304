How Wordsy Picks the Daily Word

By Jayant Singh · Published 25 October 2026

Wordsy gives every player the same word on the same day. This page explains how that word is chosen, so you know the game is fair and what to expect.

One word for everyone

The daily puzzle is the same for every player. That is what makes it fun to compare results with friends: when someone tells you they solved it in four, you are comparing the same word. Each puzzle has a number, shown as Wordsy #N. Puzzle #1 was 1 January 2025 and the number goes up by one every day.

When does it change?

A new word arrives at midnight India time (IST). If you play late at night, that is when your streak day rolls over. Each day's puzzle has its own number, so the day you play it is the day it counts.

Where the words come from

The daily word is drawn from a list of 576 five-letter words. The order is a fixed shuffle, so there are no repeats until every word on the list has been used, which is more than a year and a half of puzzles. Words that are also game commands (such as stats or share) are never used as answers, so typing a command can never be mistaken for a guess.

Your first word is a friendly one

On your first day, your daily puzzle is a simple, familiar word chosen so that new players can win their first game. Hints are unlimited on that first word. From the next day on you get the real daily word, with a small number of hints per word.

Unlimited mode

Once you have finished the daily word, you can keep playing in Unlimited mode: random words from the same list, as many as you like. Unlimited games do not count towards your daily streak. You cannot start an Unlimited game while today's daily word is still in progress, which keeps the daily puzzle the main event.

Fair play

  • There are no hidden changes to the word during the day.
  • Everyone has six guesses.
  • If you give up, the game shows you the word and counts it as a loss in your stats.
