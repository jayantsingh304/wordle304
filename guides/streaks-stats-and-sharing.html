Streaks, Stats and Sharing Your Results in Wordsy

By Jayant Singh · Published 25 October 2026

Part of the fun of a daily puzzle is the small record you build over time. Wordsy keeps that record for you inside WhatsApp. Here is what it tracks and how to use it.

Your stats

Send stats (or tap My stats) after you finish a game. You will see:

  • Played: how many games you have finished.
  • Win %: the share of those games you solved.
  • Current streak: how many days in a row you have won the daily word.
  • Best streak: your longest run so far.
  • Guess distribution: a small bar chart showing how many games you solved in 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 guesses.

If you have not finished any games yet, the game will say so, and ask you to play one first.

How streaks work

  • Only daily word wins count towards a streak. Unlimited games never change it.
  • Win today's word and your streak goes up by one.
  • If you win on consecutive days, the streak keeps growing. If the last daily win was before yesterday, the streak shown is zero, because the chain has been broken.
  • Losing a daily word or skipping a day ends the streak. Your best streak is kept.

The day changes at midnight India time, so a late-night game can be on either side of the line. If you care about a long streak, play earlier in the day.

Reading your distribution

The bars show where your games finish. If most of your wins are at 4 or 5, you are using your guesses well. If a lot of them are at 6, you may be wasting early guesses, so try the tips in our strategy guide. If you win a lot at 2 or 3, congratulations: you are probably lucky or using an excellent opener, as covered in best starting words.

Sharing a result without spoilers

After a game, send share (or tap Share result). Wordsy sends you a card that you can forward to friends and groups. An example card looks like this:

Wordsy #123 4/6

⬜🟨⬜⬜🟩
🟨⬜🟩⬜🟩
🟩🟩🟩⬜🟩
🟩🟩🟩🟩🟩

The card shows the puzzle number, how many guesses you used and the colours, but not the letters, so it never gives the answer away. If you used a hint, a 💡 is added so results stay comparable. A friend who taps the link in the card can start playing straight away.

Compare with friends

Because everyone gets the same word, the daily result is easy to compare: who solved it in fewer guesses, who used a hint, who kept a longer streak. Send your card to a group chat and see who wins today.
