How Hints Work in Wordsy

By Jayant Singh · Published 6 November 2026

A hint in Wordsy is a small nudge, not the answer. It reveals one letter of the word and leaves the rest of the puzzle to you. This guide explains what a hint shows, how many you get, and how to decide when one is worth using.

What a hint reveals

Each hint picks one letter of the answer that you have not yet placed in its correct spot (a green square). It shows that letter in its position, drawn like a row on the board, with the unknown squares shown as ？. You can use a hint at any point in a game, including before your first guess, when the whole row is still unknown and the hint shows a single letter in place.

A hint does not reveal the whole word, and it does not change the rules. Every letter you guess is still scored the same way, and the game still ends after six guesses.

How to get a hint

  • In WhatsApp: type hint (or /hint). Most game messages also carry a 💡 Hint button, so you rarely need to type it.
  • Before your first guess: the first hint still works on the empty board. Use it if you want a starting clue.
  • With no game running: hint asks you to send a word first, because a hint belongs to a game.

How many hints you get

Hints are counted per word, not per day:

  • Your first word is unlimited. On your very first day you get an easier word, and you can use as many hints as you like on it. The hint message says so: Hints are unlimited on your first word.
  • Every other word gives you 2 hints. Once you have used both, the 💡 Hint button disappears for that word. If you ask again, Wordsy tells you that you have used all 2 hints for this word and encourages you to keep guessing.
  • A new word starts fresh. When you start a new game, whether it is today's daily word or an Unlimited round, it gets its own hints.

The number of hints per word is a game setting and may change. The rule that applies to you is the one the bot shows when you use a hint.

When a hint is worth using

A hint helps most when you are stuck on where a letter goes rather than which letters are in the word. Some sensible times to use one:

  • After two or three guesses, when you have a few yellow letters but cannot find where they belong.
  • When the board is mostly grey and you are short of ideas. One revealed letter can open up the rest of the word.
  • On your first word, where hints are unlimited. Use as many as you like while you learn how the game feels.

A hint is less useful before you have tried a sensible second or third guess. Those guesses teach you more about the word than a single letter does, and they cost nothing.

Hints and your streak

Your streak counts daily wins, so using a hint does not change it. When you share a result, a game where you used a hint shows a 💡, so anyone you share with can see that you had help. If you prefer a clean result, simply leave the hints alone.

Learning to solve without hints

Use hints to learn patterns, not just to finish the word. After you get a hint, look at which letter it revealed and ask why it sits in that spot. Over a few weeks you will need fewer hints.
