Play Wordsy

Guess the hidden 5-letter word in six tries. Green = right spot, yellow = wrong spot, grey = not in the word.

How this page works

The daily puzzle is the same for everyone on the same calendar day (by your device date) and changes at midnight. Practice mode picks a random word as often as you like. Your streak and statistics are stored only in your own browser; nothing is sent to our server. Wordsy accepts about 12,000 common English five-letter words as guesses, and the answers come from a smaller, hand-picked list of familiar words.

New to the game? Read how to play, the best starting words and our solving strategy. Also see how repeated letters are scored.
