This policy covers donations made to Wordsy (operated by Jayant Singh, trading as Life Guru, Bengaluru, India).

Donations

Donations are voluntary and are normally not refundable. Wordsy is free to play, and a donation does not buy anything in the game.

When we refund

We refund the full amount if:

  • you were charged more than once for the same donation,
  • you were charged a wrong amount, or
  • the payment was made without your permission.

Write to jayant.singh@lifeguru24.in within 7 days of the payment with the date, the amount and the payment ID. We reply within 2 working days. An approved refund goes back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

Cancellation

You can cancel any time before you complete the payment by closing the payment window. A donation is a single payment; there are no subscriptions to cancel.
