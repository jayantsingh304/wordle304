Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about playing Wordsy, hints, streaks, your data and who is behind it. Still stuck? See how to play or contact us.

What is Wordsy?

Wordsy is a free daily 5-letter word game that you play inside WhatsApp. You get six guesses to find a hidden word, and after each guess coloured squares show how close you are.

Do I need to install an app or create an account?

No. If you have WhatsApp you can play. Send hi to +91 99015 45349 and the game starts. There is no sign-up, password or download.

Is Wordsy free?

Yes. The game is free to play. If you enjoy it, you can leave a voluntary donation after a game, but nothing in the game is locked behind a payment.

How many guesses do I get?

Six. Each guess must be a 5-letter word. After each guess, a green square means the right letter in the right spot, a yellow square means the letter is in the word but in a different spot, and a grey square means the letter is not in the word.

When does the daily word change?

A new daily word arrives at midnight India time (IST). Everyone gets the same word on the same day.

Can I play more than once a day?

Yes. After you finish the daily word you can play Unlimited mode, which gives you a random word each time, as often as you like.

What are hints and how do they work?

Type hint or tap the 💡 Hint button after your first guess. Wordsy reveals one letter of the word in its correct spot. Each word has a small number of hints, and your very first word has unlimited hints.

How do streaks work?

A streak counts how many days in a row you have won the daily word. Unlimited games do not count. If your last daily win was before yesterday, the streak shows as zero. Your best streak is always kept.

How do I share my result?

Send share or tap Share result after a game. Wordsy sends a card with your guess count and coloured squares, but no letters, so you can forward it to friends without spoiling the answer.

Why did one of my double letters turn grey?

A letter can only be matched as many times as it appears in the answer. If you type two of a letter and the word has only one, one square will be grey. Our guide on repeated letters has examples.

What commands can I use?

hint, stats, share, giveup, menu and rules. You can type them with or without a slash, for example /hint. Send rules or ? to see the instructions again.

Is Wordsy connected to Wordle or The New York Times?

No. Wordsy is an independent game made by Life Guru. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by The New York Times Company.

How does Wordsy make money?

Wordsy is run by one person. It is supported by voluntary donations and by advertising on this website, which is shown by Google AdSense.

What data do you keep about me?

Your WhatsApp number, your profile name, your messages to the game and your game statistics, so that the game can reply to you and remember your progress. See our privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Send delete my data to the game's WhatsApp number. Your games and statistics are erased immediately and you will get a confirmation.
